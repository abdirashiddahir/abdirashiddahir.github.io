Bio

I am an Advanced Mobility Engineer in the Department of Technology at HNTB Corporation in Columbus, Ohio, where I work on electric vehicle deployment, intelligent transportation systems, and connected and automated vehicles. I build geospatial tools that estimate how many charging stations are needed to certify alternative fuel corridors in Oklahoma and Indiana as fully built out under the National Electric Vehicle Infrastructure (NEVI) Formula Program, and I am developing a Connected Construction Work Zone tool to support intelligent transportation systems planning in Ohio.

I received my PhD in Geography from The Ohio State University in 2025, specializing in transportation planning and sustainable energy systems, advised by Huyen Le. My dissertation examined a just transition to electric vehicles in Columbus. Because that transition runs through transportation, energy infrastructure, and climate at once, I drew on transportation science, economic philosophy, econometrics, and complexity science, using qualitative interviews, surveys, and agent-based models to conceptualize, measure, and model it. The work speaks to two of the National Academies' grand challenges: curbing climate change while adapting to its impacts, and building efficient, healthy, and sustainable cities.

Beyond the dissertation, I have studied how bicycle infrastructure affects property values across 11 US cities, and, at the University of Seoul, where I earned an MSc in Transportation Engineering, how Asian cities differ from North American and European ones in the era of knowledge-based services. With researchers at the Marron Institute of Urban Management at New York University, I helped build the Transit Costs Project database of more than 500 transit projects in over 40 countries to understand what drives infrastructure costs.

I grew up in Somalia and trained as a civil engineer at Debre Birhan University in Ethiopia, then worked as an infrastructure engineer with World Vision International and as a climate journalist covering East Africa.
